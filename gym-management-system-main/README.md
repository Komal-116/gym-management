# GYM MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

## ABOUT THE PROJECT
The GYM Management System is a web-based and cloud-integrated platform designed to enhance gym operations by automating key functionalities such as membership management, payment tracking, notifications, and facility usage monitoring.
This system eliminates the paper-based receipt system, preventing loss of important records, and provides an automated notification system that reminds members of their payment due dates and informs them about working and non-working days. The system also includes an admin dashboard for gym owners to manage memberships, track payments, and assign personalized workout and diet plans.
The GYM Management System aims to provide a secure, scalable, and user-friendly solution that ensures smooth operation for gym owners, trainers, and members.

## OBJECTIVES:
- Digitization of Payment & Membership Records – Eliminate manual receipt handling and store all transactions securely. 
- Automated Notifications & Alerts – Keep members informed about their fee payments, schedule changes, and gym events.
- Improved Member Experience – Provide members with a platform where they can track their progress, payment history, and attendance.
- Role-based Access Control – Define different access levels for Admins, Trainers, and Members to ensure data privacy and security.
- Data Analytics & Reporting – Enable gym owners to generate financial reports, member attendance reports, and billing statements.

## EXISTING SYSTEM
Currently, many gyms rely on traditional paper-based systems for membership records, payments, and communication. This approach has several limitations:
- Manual Record-Keeping Issues – Difficult to track member payments, leading to lost or misplaced receipts.
- No Automated Notifications – Members must physically inquire about due payments and schedules.
- Time-Consuming Process – Trainers and staff spend too much time on administrative tasks instead of focusing on training members.
- Lack of Digital Reports – Gym owners cannot easily generate reports for financial planning and business insights.

## PROPOSED SYSTEM
The GYM Management System provides an efficient digital platform that solves the above issues by offering automation, security, and efficiency. The proposed system includes:
- Digital Payment & Receipt Management – Members can pay fees online, and receipts are automatically generated.
- Automated Fee Reminders – The system sends email/SMS notifications for due and overdue payments.
- Member Profile Management – Each member has a digital profile that includes attendance tracking, fee status, and workout plans.
- Trainer & Admin Dashboard – Trainers can assign workout and diet plans, while Admins can manage subscriptions and reports.
- Mobile-Friendly Interface – The platform is responsive and can be accessed via smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

## PROJECT MODULES
The project is divided into three major modules:

### Admin Module
- Manage Members – Add, update, delete member details.
- Assign Fee Packages – Create and modify membership plans.
- Generate Payment Receipts – Issue digital invoices and store records.
- Send Notifications – Automate reminders for fee payments and gym schedules.
- Report Generation – Export attendance, financial, and membership reports.
- Supplement & Diet Management – Admins can integrate a diet plan and supplement store.

### Member Module
- View Payment Receipts – Members can check and download their payment records.
- Track Attendance & Workout Plans – View assigned training sessions and diet plans.
- Receive Notifications – Get alerts for due payments, workout schedules, and gym events.

### User Module
- Search Gym Records – Users can view available facilities and trainer details.
- Book Appointments – Members can schedule personal training sessions.
